A few helpful answers.

Where are my notes and reading progress stored?

Reading progress, notes, and bookmarks are currently stored locally on your device. Cloud sync is not currently available. Uninstalling the app can remove this local data.

Does Bible reading work offline?

The bundled KJV and WEB Bible texts can be read offline. AI verse explanations require an internet connection.

How do AI explanations work?

Verse analysis uses AI to help explore a passage. These explanations can make mistakes and should be read alongside Scripture and trusted study resources.

How can I manage an Apple subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Select the app to view the subscription options available through Apple.
