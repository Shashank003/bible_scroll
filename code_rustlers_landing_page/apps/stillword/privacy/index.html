Scope and operator

This policy explains how Code Rustlers, a sole proprietorship based in India, handles information in connection with the Stillword iOS app, its online verse-analysis service, and our website at coderustlers.com. Code Rustlers is responsible for the processing described here, except where a provider independently determines how it uses information under its own terms. Contact us at contact@coderustlers.com. This notice explains our practices; it does not replace a separate consent where consent is required.

What stays on your device

The app stores bookmarks, favorites, personal notes, reading position, completed chapters, reading-plan progress, devotional completion, activity or streak information, appearance preferences, and onboarding status on your device. The current app has no account-based cloud sync for this information. Ordinary offline reading does not send your full reading history or notes to our AI backend. Operating-system backups may include app data according to your device settings and Apple’s practices; those backups are not a Code Rustlers sync service.

Apple purchases and RevenueCat

Apple handles purchase billing and payment credentials. We do not receive your full payment-card details. The RevenueCat SDK processes purchase and entitlement information to determine access, retrieve available packages, and restore subscriptions. This can include an SDK-generated app-user identifier, transaction or receipt information, product identifiers, subscription status, expiration dates, and technical app or device information. These records can remain with Apple or RevenueCat after the app is removed. We do not currently attach your notes, bookmarks, or Bible reading history to RevenueCat subscriber attributes.

What happens when you request AI analysis

The app sends a canonical verse identifier and the selected translation to our backend. The backend looks up the Bible passage and nearby verses in its own copy of the Bible data, then sends the passage, reference, translation, context, and study instructions to Google’s Gemini API. It returns generated explanations and related verse identifiers to the app. This flow does not send your personal notes, favorites list, or complete reading history. A requested passage can still reveal something about your interests, including religious interests. AI analysis is optional: use it only if you are comfortable with that request being processed.

Google’s handling of AI requests

Google processes AI prompts and generated responses under the Gemini API terms and applicable privacy terms. Treatment depends on the service tier and region. Under unpaid-service terms, Google may use inputs and outputs to improve its products, and human reviewers may review them. Paid-service terms provide different data-use treatment. Do not assume that AI requests are never retained or reviewed by the provider. Do not submit confidential, personal, health, or counseling information. We send Bible passages and study instructions rather than your personal notes. AI access is limited to adults and supported regions. Where Google requires paid API services for a region, access must use that service tier.

Caching, technical records, and retention

We do not maintain an application-level database or cache of generated AI explanations. Passage requests and responses are processed in memory to provide the requested result, and responses are marked no-store. Short-lived request timestamps support usage limits. Infrastructure providers may process connection metadata, IP addresses, security events, and operational logs. Local app information remains until you remove it or uninstall the app, subject to device backups. Purchase records follow Apple’s and RevenueCat’s retention practices. Support correspondence is kept as needed to answer the request, resolve related issues, and satisfy applicable legal obligations. Technical records are retained only for operational, security, or legal needs; provider retention is governed by the applicable service configuration and terms.

The studio website

Our website provides studio information, app resources, support, and legal documents. It does not require an account. Ordinary web requests disclose technical information such as IP address, browser headers, requested page, and time of access to the hosting infrastructure. Google Fonts supplies website fonts and receives the network information needed to serve those files. The site does not currently set optional analytics or advertising cookies. If optional measurement or advertising technologies are introduced, we will provide the relevant notice and obtain consent before using them where required.

Sharing and external destinations

We use Apple for App Store billing, RevenueCat for subscription entitlements, Google Gemini for requested AI explanations, and Railway as the backend hosting platform. These providers receive information needed for their respective functions; they do not all receive every category of data. Railway processes service traffic and infrastructure information for hosting and security. We may disclose information where required by law, to protect rights or security, or in connection with a lawful business transfer subject to appropriate protections. We do not sell personal information or currently share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. If that changes, we will give the required notices and choices first. Information you choose to share through iOS goes to your selected destination under that destination’s terms.

Analytics, crash reporting, and optional tracking

Where enabled, analytics and crash-reporting features help us understand which screens and functions work well and diagnose failures. Relevant data can include app version, device model, operating system, language, approximate region derived from network information, event times, feature interactions, performance measurements, crash stack traces, and a pseudonymous installation or session identifier. We do not include personal notes, journal-style content, or a complete reading history in analytics payloads. Diagnostic data can still be personal data and is handled accordingly.

The current app does not include a dedicated third-party analytics or advertising SDK. We will identify any additional provider and update this notice before enabling new collection. Optional analytics, personalized advertising, session recording, and cross-app tracking are not authorized simply because they are mentioned here. Where required, these features stay off unless you consent. Any tracking requiring Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission must also receive that permission. Refusing optional tracking does not remove purchased access. Contact us for privacy choices; an in-app preference will be provided if optional collection is enabled.

Support messages

If you email contact@coderustlers.com, we receive your email address and the information and attachments you send. We use them to respond, investigate the issue, and keep necessary support records. Email is handled through our email provider, Google. Please do not send passwords, API keys, payment-card details, or sensitive personal notes. We may ask for limited information needed to identify a purchase or investigate a problem.

Why information is processed

We process information to provide requested services, remember local preferences, recognize paid access, generate requested analysis, maintain reliability, prevent abuse, answer support requests, and meet legal obligations. Depending on applicable law, the legal basis may be performance of a contract, consent, a legal obligation, or our legitimate interests in operating and securing the service. Where consent is required, including for optional tracking or processing that reveals religious beliefs, we must obtain that consent separately. You may withdraw consent without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal. A general acceptance of this policy does not replace a required consent.

Your choices and deletion

You can use bundled Bible reading without requesting AI analysis. You can edit or delete notes and remove bookmarks within the app. Removing the app ordinarily removes its local data, subject to device backups and operating-system behavior. It does not cancel your Apple subscription or delete records held by third-party providers. Manage subscriptions through your Apple Account. For information actually held by us, applicable law may give you rights to access, correct, delete, restrict processing, object, withdraw consent, or request portability. Contact us at contact@coderustlers.com to make a request. You may also have a right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

Age and children

Stillword and its Gemini-powered services are intended for adults aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly invite children to provide personal information or use AI analysis. If you believe that a minor has provided personal information to us, contact contact@coderustlers.com so we can investigate and take appropriate action, including deletion where applicable.

Security and international processing

We use access controls and service-provider protections designed to safeguard information, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. Code Rustlers operates from India. Railway, Google, Apple, RevenueCat, and other necessary infrastructure providers may process information in other countries. Applicable safeguards and provider arrangements govern cross-border processing; we do not promise that all data stays in your country. Contact us to ask about the safeguards relevant to information we control. This policy does not claim a security certification or guarantee protection against every risk.
