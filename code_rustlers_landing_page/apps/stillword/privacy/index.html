Scope and operator

This policy describes the current Stillword iOS app, the local development verse-analysis backend, and the Code Rustlers resource pages. Code Rustlers is the studio name; the legal operator, address, and privacy contact are listed below and remain to be finalized. The local review site has not yet been deployed as a public service. This policy will need updating if hosting, accounts, analytics, or other features change. Reading this policy is not a substitute for a separate consent where the law requires one.

What stays on your device

The app stores bookmarks, favorites, personal notes, reading position, completed chapters, reading-plan progress, devotional completion, activity or streak information, appearance preferences, and onboarding status on your device. The current app has no account-based cloud sync for this information. Ordinary offline reading does not send your full reading history or notes to our AI backend. Operating-system backups may include app data according to your device settings and Apple’s practices; those backups are not a Code Rustlers sync service.

Apple purchases and RevenueCat

Apple handles purchase billing and payment credentials. We do not receive your full payment-card details. The RevenueCat SDK processes purchase and entitlement information to determine access, retrieve available packages, and restore subscriptions. This can include an SDK-generated app-user identifier, transaction or receipt information, product identifiers, subscription status, expiration dates, and technical app or device information. These records can remain with Apple or RevenueCat after the app is removed. We do not currently attach your notes, bookmarks, or Bible reading history to RevenueCat subscriber attributes.

What happens when you request AI analysis

The app sends a canonical verse identifier and the selected translation to our backend. The backend looks up the Bible passage and nearby verses in its own copy of the Bible data, then sends the passage, reference, translation, context, and study instructions to Google’s Gemini API. It returns generated explanations and related verse identifiers to the app. This flow does not send your personal notes, favorites list, or complete reading history. A requested passage can still reveal something about your interests, including religious interests. AI analysis is optional: use it only if you are comfortable with that request being processed.

Google’s handling of AI requests

Google handles submitted prompts and generated responses under the Gemini API terms and its applicable privacy terms. Data use differs between paid and unpaid services. Under unpaid-service terms, Google may use inputs and outputs to improve services, and human reviewers may review them; paid-service terms provide different treatment. We have not confirmed the project’s production billing tier in this local draft and do not promise that Google never retains, reviews, or uses requests. No private journal, health, counseling, or other sensitive personal details should be sent. AI features are intended for adults aged 18 or older and must also respect Google’s region and service restrictions.

Caching, technical records, and retention

Our current backend does not save AI requests or responses in a database or cache, and responses are marked no-store. It processes content in memory while generating an explanation. Its request logger is disabled. For local abuse control, it keeps request timestamps in memory for a rolling one-minute limit; those timestamps are cleared when the server restarts. Requests necessarily involve network information such as an IP address. These local design choices do not prevent Apple, RevenueCat, Google, network providers, or a future hosting provider from retaining their own operational records. Any production logging or retention settings must be assessed and reflected here before deployment.

The studio website

The current static website does not contain an analytics SDK, advertising pixel, contact form, tracking cookie, or account system. Its stylesheet requests font files from Google Fonts. Google receives network information associated with those requests, such as IP address and browser headers, under its own policy. A visitor can block remote fonts and use the built-in fallback fonts. The local preview server may record ordinary request paths, timestamps, and connection details in its terminal. A future public hosting provider may process access and security logs; its identity and retention practices must be added when hosting is selected.

Sharing and external destinations

Information is disclosed to Apple and RevenueCat for purchases and to Google for requested AI generation, as described above. We do not sell personal information or currently include advertising-tracking integrations in this codebase. We may need to disclose information we actually hold to comply with a legal obligation or protect service security. If you use the iOS share sheet, the app sends the verse or other material you choose to your selected destination. That destination’s privacy practices apply. External links also lead to services we do not control.

Why information is processed

We process the information needed to provide requested reading tools, keep local preferences, confirm purchased access, generate requested explanations, prevent misuse, and respond to any support messages you choose to send. Depending on the applicable law and the particular processing, the legal basis may be performance of a contract, a legitimate interest in operating and securing the service, a legal obligation, or your consent. Religious interests can receive additional legal protection; we will obtain any consent required for processing that data before a public AI launch. We do not treat acceptance of a general privacy policy as blanket permission for every use.

Your choices and deletion

You can use bundled Bible reading without requesting AI analysis. You can edit or delete notes and remove bookmarks within the app. Removing the app ordinarily removes its local data, subject to device backups and operating-system behavior. It does not cancel your Apple subscription or delete records held by third-party providers. Manage subscriptions through your Apple Account. For information actually held by us, applicable law may give you rights to access, correct, delete, restrict processing, object, withdraw consent, or request portability. Contact the operator using the details below once finalized. You may also have a right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

Age and children

The Gemini-powered analysis service is intended for adults aged 18 or older. We do not intentionally invite minors to use that service. The final app audience, age-related controls, App Store rating, and any consent requirements must be confirmed before launch. We do not knowingly seek children’s personal information. If a child’s information has been sent to us, a parent or guardian should contact the operator so we can assess and address it.

Security and international processing

Local storage depends in part on the security of your device and operating system. The current demo backend uses a shared development token and local-network HTTP; it is not a public production service. Production deployment requires HTTPS, suitable authentication, access controls, and a review of provider configurations. No system is completely secure. Apple, RevenueCat, and Google may process information in countries other than your country of residence under their applicable arrangements. We have not yet selected a public hosting region and do not claim that all processing stays in a particular country.
