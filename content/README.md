# Discover content provenance

The app bundles `BibleScroll/discover.json`: 30 Scripture studies, 10 original reflections, and 10 Bible facts. Scripture quotations are resolved from the bundled KJV library, not copied into commentary fields.

All commentary, reflections, and fact summaries were written specifically for this app. Reflections are labeled Original reflection; they are not historical quotations or excerpts from copyrighted devotional books. Each entry records canonical verse IDs in sources. Fact summaries are grounded in the cited narrative or letter passages; source buttons display those passages verbatim from the selected library.

The curated studies contain explanation, passage context, application, and suggested related passages. These explanations represent editorial interpretation, not additional Bible text. There is no Greek/Hebrew lexicon or AI generation service connected. For passages without a curated study, the analysis view provides actual surrounding verses and general reading/reflection questions.

Validation checks ensure exactly 50 unique cards, a 30/10/10 split, nonempty text, resolvable Scripture citations, mixed ordering, and no placeholder language. Future translations must use the same canonical book/chapter/verse IDs. Review editorial content when publishing or changing the intended theological scope.
