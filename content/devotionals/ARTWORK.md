## December prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: original editorial cover illustration for an offline Christian daily devotional iPhone app, December series 'Light in the Waiting'. No text or lettering anywhere. Wide landscape composition, approximately 3:2. A softly glowing candle on a warm limestone windowsill, a simple olive branch, distant rounded hills seen through an arched window at first dawn. Refined hand-painted gouache and watercolor on subtly textured ivory paper, generous uncluttered negative space, quiet spiritual warmth, sophisticated book-cover art, muted parchment cream, sand, copper, soft sage and deep umber. Restrained and poetic, soft edges, tactile pigment, not a photograph, not clip art, no people, no frame, no UI, no logo. Main imagery in upper two thirds with a light cream lower edge that can fade into a reading page.

## January prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: original editorial cover illustration for a Christian daily devotional iPhone app, January series 'Faith for the Everyday'. No text or lettering anywhere. Wide landscape composition approximately 3:2. A quiet winding footpath beside a still river through softly layered hills at sunrise, a small olive tree in the left foreground, a pale warm sun low in an ivory sky. Refined hand-painted gouache and watercolor on textured paper, uncluttered poetic landscape, sophisticated devotional book-cover art. Muted sage, warm cream, sand, subtle terracotta and deep umber, soft natural edges and tactile pigment. Calm hopeful mood without visual clutter. Not a photograph, no people, no buildings, no frame, no UI or logos. Main landscape in upper two thirds with a light cream lower edge that can fade into a reading page.
